Description

LUMI CLEAR™ Acne & Blemish Care Cream is a skincare concept developed around the needs of acne-prone, blemish-affected and uneven-looking skin.

Its philosophy is built on Clarity, Radiance and Balance, with an emphasis on visible blemishes, post-acne marks, skin comfort and natural-looking radiance.

Ingredient Concept

Proposed ingredients, subject to confirmation against the final manufactured product: Salicylic Acid, Azelaic Acid, Niacinamide (Vitamin B3), Zinc PCA, Alpha Arbutin, Green Tea Extract, Centella Asiatica Extract, Licorice Root Extract, Panthenol (Vitamin B5), Ceramide NP and Allantoin.

These are proposed formulation ingredients, not a confirmed product ingredient declaration.

How to Use

Follow the directions supplied with the final manufactured formulation. As general guidance, gently cleanse your face and pat dry, apply the manufacturer-recommended amount, avoid eyes and lips, use a moisturizer if needed, and wear broad-spectrum sunscreen during the daytime.

Application frequency should follow the manufacturer-approved instructions.

Precautions

For external use only. Avoid the eyes and mucous membranes. Do not apply to broken or severely irritated skin without professional advice. Discontinue use if significant irritation occurs. Consult a healthcare professional if using prescription acne treatments.

Storage

Keep out of reach of children. Store according to the final manufacturer’s instructions.

Packaging

Distinctive fresh-green botanical packaging with white and gold details, round clear-and-gold jar, and a pale-green cream concept. Net weight: 18g. Country of origin: Thailand.

Frequently Asked Questions

What skin concerns is LUMI CLEAR™ intended for?

It is positioned for blemish-prone skin, visible post-acne marks, uneven tone and skin-barrier care.

Is the full ingredient list confirmed?

No. The listed key ingredients are proposed and must be checked against the final manufactured formulation.

How often should I apply it?

Use only at the frequency stated on the manufacturer-approved packaging.

Should I use sunscreen?

Yes, broad-spectrum sunscreen is recommended as part of a daytime skincare routine.
