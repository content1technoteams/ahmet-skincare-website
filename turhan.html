Description

TURHAN Skin Lightening Cream is a cosmetic skincare product designed for those looking to improve the appearance of uneven skin tone and achieve a brighter, smoother-looking complexion.

Its beauty-focused positioning centres on the appearance of dark spots, blemishes and visible unevenness in skin tone.

With a compact 20g presentation and distinctive packaging, TURHAN brings a premium touch to your personal skincare collection.

Ingredients

Please refer to the original product packaging for the complete, confirmed ingredient list. No full verified INCI list was provided in the creative brief.

How to Use

Follow the directions provided on the original product packaging. Before the first use, perform a patch test on a small area of skin. Avoid contact with the eyes and discontinue use if irritation occurs.

During daytime, use an appropriate broad-spectrum sunscreen as part of your skincare routine.

Precautions

For external use only. Keep out of reach of children. Check the original product packaging for full precautions and product-specific directions.

Storage

Store according to the instructions on the original packaging.

Packaging

Premium burgundy and gold packaging with a clear faceted jar and distinctive TURHAN identity. Net weight: 20g.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this product suitable for all skin types?

Suitability varies by skin type. Perform a patch test before use, especially if you have sensitive skin.

How long does it take to see results?

Results vary depending on skin type, concerns and consistent use.

Can it be used with other skincare products?

It may complement your skincare routine. Avoid combining strong active ingredients without professional guidance.

Is this product safe for daily use?

Follow the manufacturer’s instructions. Discontinue use if irritation occurs and apply sunscreen during the day.
